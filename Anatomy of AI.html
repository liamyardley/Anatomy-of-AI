Anatomy of AI

"AI" is often taken to mean ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, as if it arrived a few years ago. The field is decades old. Alan Turing asked whether machines could think in 1950, the term "artificial intelligence" was coined for a workshop at Dartmouth in 1956, and the idea goes back further still: in 1843 Ada Lovelace wondered whether Babbage's Analytical Engine might one day compose music.
